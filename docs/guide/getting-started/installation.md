# Installation

Nolex is available in the Chrome Web Store. Installation takes less than 30 seconds.

## Step 1: Open Chrome Web Store

Go to the Nolex page in Chrome Web Store:

[**Install Nolex**](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/nolex/chebnmpkokgdhdcmlfooilohcanlpppp)

Or search for "Nolex" in the Chrome Web Store.

## Step 2: Click "Add to Chrome"

Click the blue **"Add to Chrome"** button in the top right corner of the page.

## Step 3: Confirm Permissions

Chrome will show a popup asking you to confirm the extension's permissions:

| Permission | Why it's needed |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Storage** | To save your settings and custom patterns locally on your device |
| **Access to all websites** | To scan files and clipboard on any AI platform you use |

Click **"Add extension"** to confirm.

## Step 4: Verify Installation

After installation, you'll see the Nolex icon in your browser toolbar (top right).

- **Blue shield icon** = Nolex is active and protecting you
- Click the icon to open the popup menu

> **Tip:** If you don't see the icon, click the puzzle piece icon in the toolbar and pin Nolex.

## That's It!

Nolex is now active. No configuration, no account, no setup required.

Every time you upload a file or paste text into an AI chatbot, Nolex will automatically check for sensitive data.

Chrome automatically updates extensions. You don't need to do anything.

To check your current version:
1. Click the Nolex icon in the toolbar
2. The version number is displayed in the popup header
